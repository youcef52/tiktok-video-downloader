1. What is DMCA?

The Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) is a United States copyright law that provides guidelines for online service providers in cases of copyright infringement. TikVultix complies with all DMCA requirements and takes prompt action when valid infringement claims are submitted.

2. Filing a DMCA Complaint

📝 Required Information

If you believe that any content available on our website infringes your copyright, please submit a written notification containing the following information:

  • 1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized agent
  • 2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed
  • 3. Identification of the infringing material and its location on our website (URL)
  • 4. Your contact information: full name, email address, phone number
  • 5. A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner
  • 6. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, under penalty of perjury
⚠️ Important: Knowingly submitting a false DMCA claim is illegal and may result in legal consequences. Please only submit claims for content you genuinely believe infringes your rights.

3. How We Handle DMCA Notices

⏱️ Our Response Process

  • Step 1: We receive your DMCA notice via email
  • Step 2: Our team reviews the claim within 48 business hours
  • Step 3: If valid, we immediately remove or disable access to the infringing content
  • Step 4: We notify the user who uploaded the content (if applicable)
  • Step 5: The user may file a counter-notification if they believe the removal was mistaken

4. Counter-Notification

If you believe that content you uploaded was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may file a counter-notification. Your counter-notification must include:

  • Your physical or electronic signature
  • Identification of the material that was removed and its former location
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the material was removed by mistake
  • Your name, address, and phone number
  • Consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court in your district

5. Repeat Infringers

In accordance with the DMCA and other applicable laws, TikVultix may terminate, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of users who are repeat infringers. We maintain a record of all DMCA notices and take strict action against repeat violations.

6. Content Not Stored on Our Servers

✅ Important Notice: TikVultix does not store any videos, files, or user content on our servers. We provide a tool that allows users to download videos directly from TikTok. We are not responsible for content hosted on third-party platforms, including TikTok.

7. Limitation of Liability

TikVultix is a tool for downloading publicly available content from TikTok. We do not host, upload, or store any copyrighted material on our servers. Users are solely responsible for ensuring they have the right to download and use the content. TikVultix is not liable for any copyright infringement committed by users.
