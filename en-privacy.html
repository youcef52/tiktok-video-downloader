1. Information We May Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it. What may be automatically collected is only non-personal technical data:

📊 Log Data

When you visit the site, our server may automatically collect technical data such as:

  • IP address (anonymized and partially encrypted)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system (Windows, iOS, Android)
  • Pages of the site you visit
  • Time, date, and duration of visit

This data is used only to diagnose technical issues and improve site performance, not to identify you personally.

🍪 Cookies

We use only two types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for site operation such as saving language preferences and quality settings.
  • Analytics cookies: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site in aggregate, not personally.

You can disable cookies at any time in your browser settings without affecting your basic use of the site.

2. What We Never Collect

✅ We confirm categorically that we never collect: your real name, email address, phone number, credit card data, the links you paste and download, the videos you download, or any other sensitive data. TikVultix doesn't need any of this information to function.

3. How We Use Information

🎯 Permitted Uses Only

The technical data we may collect is used exclusively for:

  • Operating the site and ensuring its stability and performance
  • Diagnosing and fixing technical issues
  • Analyzing traffic in aggregate to improve service
  • Protection against cyberattacks and abuse

4. Third-Party Advertising

The site may display ads from third-party companies such as Google AdSense. These companies may use their own cookies to display personalized ads based on your general interests.

💡 Note: You can control Google ads through the Google Ads Settings page, or install the "Opt out of Google Analytics" browser extension to completely disable ad tracking.

5. Information Sharing

🚷 We Never Sell or Share Your Personal Data

We do not sell, trade, or rent any personal information to third parties under any circumstances. The only possible exceptions:

  • Legal compliance: If required by court order or applicable law.
  • Rights protection: To defend TikVultix's or users' rights against any threat.
  • Service providers: We may share anonymous technical data with cloud service providers solely for site operation purposes.

6. User Rights (GDPR)

⚖️ Your Guaranteed Rights

  • Right of Access: Know whether we hold any data about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct any inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of any data about you ("Right to be Forgotten").
  • Right to Object: Object to processing your data for certain purposes.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive a copy of your data in a readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: support@tikvultix.com

7. Data Security

  • All connections encrypted with HTTPS/TLS
  • Servers protected by advanced firewalls
  • Security procedures reviewed periodically
  • Videos and links are never stored on our servers
⚠️ Note: Despite our commitment to the highest security standards, no method of transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed 100% secure. We advise you not to share sensitive information through any website.

8. Children Under 13

TikVultix is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

Our site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy policies or content of those sites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external site you visit.
