❓ Common Problems and Solutions on Android

Here are the most common problems you might face and how to easily solve them:

The video opens in the browser instead of downloading

Long-press on the video inside the browser and select "Download Video" or "Save Video". Or wait until the download finishes in the background and look for it in the Downloads folder.

I can't find the video after downloading

Open the Files app (File Manager) and look in the Downloads or DCIM folder. You can also open Google Photos and you'll find the video there if photo sync is enabled.

Download is very slow

Check your internet connection. If you're on mobile data, try connecting to WiFi for better speed. Download speed depends on your connection, not the site.
