🎬 What Are TikTok Stories and Why Do They Disappear?

TikTok Stories are short vertical video clips that users share temporarily — similar to Instagram and Snapchat Stories. The key difference is they automatically disappear after 24 hours with no built-in save option in the app.

TikTok Stories differ from regular videos: they're fully vertical (9:16), very short in duration, and typically more casual and spontaneous. Many creators share exclusive behind-the-scenes content in their Stories that vanishes forever — TikVultix is the solution to preserve that content.
