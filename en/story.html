❓ Common Story Download Issues & Fixes

"Story not found" error

This usually means the story has already expired (more than 24 hours old) or was deleted by the creator. Stories are only downloadable while they are still live on TikTok.

Story link looks different from a regular TikTok link

That's normal. Story links often contain /story/ in the URL. As long as you copied it from the Share button on the story itself, TikVultix will recognize and process it correctly.

Can I download stories from private accounts?

No. TikTok does not allow access to stories from private accounts through any external tool. Both the account and the story must be public to download.

Downloaded story is a video but the original had music

Some stories contain copyrighted music that TikTok strips when downloaded through any external tool. This is a platform‑level restriction, not a TikVultix limitation. Stories with original audio download with full sound.
