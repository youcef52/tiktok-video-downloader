❓ Common Android Issues & Fixes

Video opens in browser instead of downloading

Long‑press the video and select "Download video" or "Save video". Alternatively, wait a few seconds — some browsers complete the download in the background silently.

I can't find the video after downloading

Open your Files app and navigate to the Downloads folder. On Samsung devices, it may also appear in My Files > Internal Storage > Download. Google Photos often indexes it automatically too.

Download is extremely slow

Check your internet connection. Slow speeds are usually caused by weak WiFi or congested mobile data. Switch to a 5 GHz WiFi network if available.

Website says "Link invalid"

Make sure you copied the full TikTok URL (it should look like https://vm.tiktok.com/... or https://www.tiktok.com/@user/video/...). Remove any extra text before pasting.
