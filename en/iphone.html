❓ Common iPhone Issues & Fixes

Video doesn't save to Photos

After the video loads in Safari, tap the Share icon at the bottom center, then scroll and select "Save Video". If you don't see the option, long‑press the video and choose "Save to Photos" instead.

I get a "Cannot download" error

Check that you are on WiFi or stable cellular data. Also ensure your iPhone has enough free storage — HD videos can be 50–150 MB each. Go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage to check.

Video downloads but looks blurry

TikVultix always fetches the highest available quality. If the video appears blurry, the original upload on TikTok may be low resolution. Try a different video to confirm the quality.

Safari asks for permission to download

This is normal iOS behavior. Tap "Allow" when Safari prompts you. You can manage download permissions in Settings > Safari > Downloads.
