❓ Common MP3 Download Issues & Fixes

MP3 file is silent or has no sound

Some TikTok videos use copyrighted music that may be muted on certain servers. Try a different video to verify, or ensure the original TikTok has audio enabled when you copy the link.

Download starts but file doesn't save

Check your browser's download settings. On Chrome, go to Settings > Downloads and ensure the download location is accessible. On iPhone, you may need to tap "Save to Files" if the MP3 doesn't appear automatically.

Audio quality sounds lower than expected

MP3 quality depends on the original TikTok upload. If the creator uploaded compressed audio, the MP3 will reflect that. TikVultix does not add any extra compression.

Can't download MP3 from private accounts

Correct. TikTok's API only allows access to public content. Private account videos cannot be downloaded in any format.
