⚙️ Required Safari Settings for Downloading

Step 1 — Photos Permission: Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Make sure Safari has permission to access Photos.

Step 2 — Download Location: Go to Settings → Safari → Downloads → Choose "Photos" or "On My iPhone" to set where files are saved.

Step 3 — Enable JavaScript: Go to Settings → Safari → Advanced → Make sure JavaScript is enabled. Without it, the site may not work correctly.

⚠️ Common issue: If the video opens in Safari instead of downloading, long-press on the video and select "Save to Photos" or "Download Linked File" from the menu that appears.
